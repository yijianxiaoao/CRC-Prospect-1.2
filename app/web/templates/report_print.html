4. Theoretical Interpretation

Screening behavior is not determined by disease risk alone. Individuals form belief structures during the information-editing phase and then compare gains and losses relative to reference points and risk preference during the evaluation phase, leading to screening intention and actual behavior. Systematic biases, health literacy, self-efficacy, service accessibility, and policy support jointly influence this process.
